================================================================================
                       TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

PRESET BUTTON CALCULATION LOGIC:

  For each preset (e.g., +$10):
  amount = Math.ceil(grandTotal / 10) * 10

  Examples:
  • Total $12.50 → +$10 = $20
  • Total $20.00 → +$10 = $20 (exact)
  • Total $20.01 → +$10 = $30

CHANGE CALCULATION:

  changeGiven = tendered_amount - grandTotal

  Examples:
  • Tendered $50, Total $23.50 → Change = $26.50
  • Tendered $25, Total $23.50 → Change = $1.50
  • Tendered $23.50, Total $23.50 → Change = $0.00

VALIDATION LOGIC:

  Complete button enabled if:
    • cashTendered !== ''
    • typeof cashTendered === 'number'
    • cashTendered >= grandTotal
    • !isConfirming

  This ensures:
    • Amount is entered
    • Amount is valid number
    • Amount is sufficient
    • Not already processing
